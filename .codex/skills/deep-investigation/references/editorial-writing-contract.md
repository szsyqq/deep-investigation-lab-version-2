# Financial investigation writing contract

Read before outlining, drafting, or structurally revising an investigation.

## Non-negotiable priority

- Accuracy and professional evidence discipline come first. Every factual detail must be traceable to an accessible source and represented within that source's actual scope.
- Readability, ease, and attraction come next and remain mandatory. They are achieved by choosing consequential facts, ordering them as a causal story, explaining terms in plain language, and using verified contrast.
- Never improve drama by inventing a scene, motive, quote, number, trend, precision, or causal link. If a useful fact cannot be verified, return to research, label the uncertainty, narrow the sentence, or omit it.
- Evidence density is not a license to dump data. Select facts that answer the section question, give the relevant denominator and comparison, and explain why they matter.

## Article-level contract

1. State one core investigation question that a reasonable reader cannot answer from the headline news alone.
2. Separate signals by time, market, actor, and causal level before presenting a contradiction.
3. Use current news, a person, or a scene as an entry only when it opens a testable question. Do not treat prominence, holdings, or quotations as proof.
4. Build a causal sequence. A vivid scene without the connecting mechanism is decoration, not narrative.
5. Distinguish company disclosure, third-party fact, attributed explanation, article analysis, and unresolved inference.
6. End with the evidence actually established. Do not replace a verdict with a generic checklist or introduce a new thesis in the epilogue.
7. Build story from verified scenes, actions, dates, documents, and attributed speech. Do not invent inner motives to make a person legible.

## Two-layer opening

- `deck` is the 引言 beneath the title. Use a compact set of facts or numbers to establish the central tension and reading value.
- `序言` is the first prose section. Tell a continuous story with a scene, actor, time, price, product, or event, then move naturally to the investigation question.
- Do not repeat the same facts mechanically in the 引言, 序言, and first chapter.
- “Brief” means economical, not incomplete. Keep enough detail to make the causal and temporal sequence intelligible.

## Section contract

Before writing a section, answer:

- What exact question does this section answer?
- What is the proposed conclusion?
- Through what mechanism would that conclusion be true?
- Which evidence IDs support it?
- What counterevidence, alternative explanation, or disclosure gap could weaken it?
- What is the narrowest conclusion justified now?
- Why must the next section follow?

If any answer is missing, return to research or outline work. Do not fill the gap with abstract prose.

## Evidence-density rules

- When a section disputes an interpretation, begin with the measurable observation: name the item, period, starting value, ending value, and comparable denominator before discussing causes.
- A financial figure must do analytical work: show its denominator, comparison, transmission path, balance-sheet consequence, or contradiction.
- An annual report section must go beyond extracting growth rates. Examine concentration, margins, cash conversion, working capital, commitments, and what management does not disclose when relevant.
- A secondary-market price is not demand by itself. Separate supply, availability, speculation, sell-through, repeat purchase, discounting, and primary-market revenue.
- A famous investor's action is a lead. Reconstruct timing, instrument, position changes, stated thesis, weakest assumption, and disconfirming evidence before using it.
- Never label market value as investment profit. Estimate gains only for transactions with disclosed quantity, cost basis, and a common valuation date; state coverage, excluded fees, missing option premiums, and whether the result is realized or unrealized.
- Explain missing data only when the absence materially changes the conclusion. If an unavailable calculation does not help the reader answer the section question, omit it from prose.
- Absence of disclosure is a limitation, not proof of absence.

## Language rules

- Prefer concrete subjects, actions, numbers, and consequences over stacked abstractions.
- Do not repeat the preceding paragraph as a meta-summary. A summary must compress several findings into a new conclusion; otherwise delete it.
- Keep paragraphs causally connected. Merge isolated setup sentences into the evidence they introduce, and make each paragraph either add a fact, explain a mechanism, test an alternative, or advance the narrative.
- Create interest through factual contrast and withheld resolution, not “竟然”“居然” or manufactured astonishment.
- Remove sentences that remain true for almost any company, add no evidence, and do not advance the argument.
- Avoid empty balance such as “可能正确，也可能错误” unless the next sentence identifies the deciding mechanism and evidence.
- Use professional, restrained subheads that name a fact, mechanism, or analytical tension.
- Do not use classroom, game, or motivational language—such as “考试”, “答题”, “闯关”, “赢了”, “输赢”, or “下一关”—to frame an ordinary business analysis. Do not rely on conversational rhetorical questions when a declarative heading or evidence-led sentence is more precise.
- Prefer the tone of a financial investigation: state the event, identify the operating implication, then define the limit of the evidence. Let the unresolved issue emerge from the record rather than repeatedly announcing it to the reader.

## Positive and negative patterns

Positive:

> 公司扩大供给后，二手溢价回落。这可以来自需求降温，也可以来自缺货缓解；同店销售、全价售罄和复购数据才能区分两种解释。

Why it works: separates two mechanisms and identifies discriminating evidence.

Negative:

> 市场永远可能正确，也可能错误，时间会给出答案。

Why it fails: universally true, contains no mechanism, evidence, or usable test.

Positive:

> 投资者提高持股并不能证明公司被低估，但其公开判断可以拆成利润基准、竞争壁垒和需求持续性三项假设，再由公司数据逐项检验。

Why it works: converts prominence into testable claims rather than authority.

Positive:

> 年报显示库存周转由102天升至123天，公司将其归因于海外运输周期和门店备货。同期经营现金流仍为正，因此现有证据支持“全球扩张变重”，但不足以证明“库存危机”。

Why it works: the numbers, attribution, counterevidence, and bounded conclusion are all visible, while the language remains understandable.

Negative:

> 库存暴增说明消费者已经厌倦LABUBU，泡泡玛特的繁荣即将结束。

Why it fails: it converts one accounting signal into demand, motive, and forecast without sell-through or consumer evidence. Dramatic readability cannot repair unsupported causality.
